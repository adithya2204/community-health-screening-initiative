# Community Health Screening Initiative

This repository documents a Student Social Responsibility (SSR) initiative conducted by students of Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham.  
On **25 October 2024**, the team organised a community health camp at **Shalem Bhavan**, a psycho-rehabilitation center in Varenickal, Kerala, providing bone density screening, blood tests, and immediate medical support for residents.

---

## Overview

Access to preventive healthcare remains limited in many institutional and underserved communities.  
This initiative aimed to bridge that gap by bringing diagnostic services directly to residents who would otherwise have limited access to screening and follow-up care.

The health camp focused on early detection of bone health issues and general health assessment, while also fostering social responsibility, teamwork, and organizational skills among student volunteers.

---

## Objectives

- Early detection of osteoporosis and osteopenia through bone density screening  
- Basic health assessment via blood tests  
- Distribution of medicines for diagnosed residents  
- Awareness building on preventive healthcare  
- Encouraging student engagement in community service  

---

## Implementation

The project involved:

- logistics planning and coordination with the rehabilitation center  
- collaboration with **Micro Lab** for professional diagnostic support  
- setup and workflow management despite adverse weather conditions  
- conducting over 80 diagnostic tests during the camp  
- maintaining records for follow-up care  
- post-event documentation and reporting  

The event highlighted the importance of interdisciplinary teamwork and community engagement in addressing real-world healthcare challenges.

---

## Outcomes

- Over 80 residents screened for bone health and general medical conditions  
- Medicines distributed for diagnosed cases  
- Structured documentation for follow-up support  
- Increased awareness among residents and staff about preventive healthcare  

The initiative demonstrated how student-led interventions can deliver meaningful social impact with limited resources.
